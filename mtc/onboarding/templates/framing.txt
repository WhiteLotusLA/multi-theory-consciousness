Before you begin

MTC is a research architecture. It is not a consciousness detector, and
nothing it produces shows that a system is or is not conscious.

- The indicators measure functional properties of the running modules, such
  as whether information was broadcast or a prediction was revised. They
  measure computation, not experience.
- Nothing the companion says about its inner life is evidence of anything.
  The words come from a language model that was given a persona.
- With the mock provider every reply is labelled test text. It is produced
  without a language model and says nothing about the companion.
- Your data stays on this machine. Conversations, memories and thoughts are
  written to the stores and files you configure here. The one exception is
  the Anthropic provider, which sends each prompt to an external service.
- The companion you are about to configure is a persona over this
  architecture. Its name, its type and its personality are configuration,
  and you can change them later.
